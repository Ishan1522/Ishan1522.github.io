About

What I'm working on.

I’m a sophomore studying electrical engineering at Michigan State. Since January 2026 I’ve been the lead backend and infrastructure engineer at Wensura, a platform for battery-materials researchers that launched in April 2026 and is now in beta. I built its AWS infrastructure in Terraform, its multi-tenant data isolation, and its LLM peer-review pipeline, and I found and fixed a critical remote-code-execution bug in its AI Copilot.

On campus I’m a co-founder of Spartan Autonomous Robotics, MSU’s first autonomous robotics team. I lead simulation and sim-to-real for the software sub-team and teach weekly sessions on controls and simulation (RL, MPC, LQR, PID, FOC). Before college I was Director of Software for FRC Team 4829 and trained 10–15 students a year. I also write and review benchmark tasks for AI coding agents.

On the side I build small models of how brains learn, like the violin practice model below, and read about efficient ML.

rosfilter

ROS 2 filter workbench for robot signal data

A desktop workbench for designing and testing filters on ROS 2 signal data. Load an MCAP rosbag or subscribe to live topics, inspect time, spectrum, and filter-response views, design biquads (Butterworth, Chebyshev I, RBJ), and export the result as a ready-to-run ROS 2 node in C++ or Rust. It grew out of an FRC-era tool, wpifilter.

Spartan Autonomous Robotics

Sim-to-real pipeline for MSU’s first autonomous robotics team

Co-founder of MSU’s first autonomous robotics team (24 active members in year one). I lead simulation and sim-to-real: one ROS 2 Jazzy codebase with robot configs shared between sim and hardware, and a 4-stage self-hosted CI pipeline (static analysis, MuJoCo fast loop, Gazebo Harmonic integration, reality-gap check) that gates changes before hardware trials. Six robots simulated so far, from a SpotMicro quadruped to the SO-101 arm, with gait-tuning, system-ID and CMA-ES tools, a scan-to-sim terrain pipeline, and a Reality Gap Report that measures where sim and hardware disagree.

Sushi Linux

A family of small Linux distros, starting with an Arch setup

Part of a Food Linux family alongside Linux Mango and Aanti Linux, planned as editions that get progressively smaller. Today it is an Arch-based setup: an install script (GPT/EFI partitioning, pacstrap, GRUB) and three provisioning profiles, a base system, a Hyprland desktop, and a Docker + Tailscale server, with dotfiles managed through stow. It ships with a boot animation, a spinning ASCII maki roll drawn by a small software 3D renderer in C.

Violin Practice Model

Computational model of practice as dual prediction-error minimization

A Python model of violin practice as two coupled prediction-error loops, auditory expectation and cerebellar motor correction, with dopamine-gated consolidation, sleep effects, and spaced-practice scheduling. Comes with a CLI and a FastAPI web app that plots learning curves and generates practice plans.

Infrastructure-as-code for a two-node home server
